# INVARIANT

### Quantitative Investment Research

> **Researching persistent structures across global markets.**

INVARIANT is an independent quantitative investment research initiative focused on global financial markets.

We combine **macroeconomic reasoning** with **systematic quantitative research** to investigate persistent relationships, market inefficiencies, and cross-asset dynamics.

Our primary focus is on **equities and commodities**, with additional research across **FX, credit, and prediction markets**.

---

## Research Philosophy

**Macro generates the question.
Quantitative research tests it.**

We begin with an economic or market observation, formulate a testable hypothesis, and investigate whether the underlying relationship survives rigorous empirical testing.

Our research process:

```text
Observation
     ↓
Hypothesis
     ↓
Data
     ↓
Model
     ↓
Robustness
     ↓
Systematic Expression
```

We care about more than backtested performance.

Research should be:

* **Economically interpretable**
* **Statistically rigorous**
* **Robust across specifications**
* **Tested out-of-sample where possible**
* **Considerate of transaction costs and implementation**
* **Reproducible**

A strategy that works only in one backtest is not a result. It is a hypothesis.

## Methodology

INVARIANT follows a hypothesis-driven research process.

We do not begin with a desired result.

Instead:

1. Identify an economically meaningful observation.
2. Translate the observation into a falsifiable hypothesis.
3. Define the relevant data and sample before testing where practical.
4. Construct appropriate statistical and quantitative tests.
5. Evaluate robustness across specifications and market regimes.
6. Separate in-sample discovery from out-of-sample evaluation.
7. Account for transaction costs, liquidity, turnover, and implementation.
8. Determine whether the result warrants systematic expression.

**Negative results are results.**

A hypothesis that fails under robust testing is more valuable than a backtest selected because it looks good.
